Numeria is a math adventure RPG for kids, hosted entirely on your own server.

Players create a young wizard, explore a world map and battle friendly monsters. Every spell is cast by answering a math question: a correct answer makes the spell hit, a wrong one fizzles and shows a short explanation of how to solve it.

### Features

- **33 math skills from grade 1 to 8**: addition and subtraction, times tables, division, rounding, fractions, decimals, order of operations, percentages, negative numbers, exponents, ratios, equations, angles, roots and the Pythagorean theorem
- **Adaptive difficulty**: each skill has its own mastery score; weak skills come up more often, questions get harder as a skill is mastered, and the next grade is previewed once the current one is solid
- **Turn-based battles** with four elements (Fire, Water, Nature, Storm), type advantages, answer streaks, critical hits, healing and shield spells
- **Five lands to explore**, each with three monsters and a guardian boss
- **Familiars**: befriend defeated monsters and bring them into battle
- **Shop, wardrobe and achievements**: potions, wands, hats and robes bought with gold earned in battle
- **Teacher dashboard** (protected by YunoHost SSO): see every player's progress, accuracy and weakest skills, lock their math grade, reset passwords and export a CSV
- Works on phones, tablets and computers; keyboard shortcuts (1-4) for answers
- No ads, no tracking, no external resources: everything stays on your server
